Business Context & Privacy Mandate
Why Unredacted Corporate Data Must Never Reach Downstream LLMs

[SLIDE_SHAPE sh6]
•   Regulatory Imperative: GDPR, GLBA, and financial data privacy prohibit sending customer/employee PII to external model APIs.
•   Confidentiality Scope: Organizational rosters, risk management policies, and third-party vendor training materials.
•   The Zero-Leakage Catch: Models fine-tuned or queried on raw text risk persistent memorization and data leakage.
•   Core Objective: Intercept and neutralize sensitive entities locally before vectorization or LLM prompt construction.

Technical Architecture & Component Choices
Engineered for 100% Local Runtimes and Maximum Reliability

[SLIDE_SHAPE sh6]
•   Word (.docx) Engine: python-docx + lxml traversing true document order (paragraphs, tables, embedded images).
•   Presentation (.pptx) Engine: python-pptx walking shapes, tables, charts, and slide speaker notes.
•   PDF & OCR Engine: PyMuPDF (fitz) for sub-second native text + pytesseract at 300 DPI for scanned pages.
•   NLP Engine: spaCy (en_core_web_sm) with customized non-person stop-word filtering + Python re engine.
•   Orchestrator: 01_Demo/main.py unifying all 5 modules with modular contracts and fresh-runtime capability.

Extraction Logic & OCR Fallback Strategy
Handling Scanned Pages, Nested Tables, and Embedded Vector Graphics

[SLIDE_SHAPE sh6]
•   DOCX Invariant: Extracts paragraphs and table cells in strict reading order with heading hierarchy tracking.
•   PPTX Traversal: Recursive walk through slide shape groups, notes, and embedded screenshots.
•   Scanned PDF Detection: PyMuPDF checks native char threshold (<30 chars triggers automatic 300 DPI image rendering).
•   OCR Quality Scoring: Tesseract word-confidence scored per block; scores <70 flagged with explicit warnings.
•   Traceability Contract: Every text unit is assigned a globally unique segment_id (<filename>#00001).

Hybrid PII Detection & Precision Tuning
Combining Deterministic Regex and Statistical Named Entity Recognition

[SLIDE_SHAPE sh6]
•   High-Precision Regex: Corporate emails, E.164 & local phone formats, IPv4 addresses, and EMP/AC IDs.
•   Statistical NER: spaCy Person Entity Recognizer with custom domain heuristic guardrails.
•   False-Positive Suppression: Eliminated corporate terms ('TPRM', 'Ironclad', 'Appendix') and policy numbers.
•   Boundary Overlap Resolver: Strict priority deduplication prevents email sub-strings from misclassifying as names.
•   Confidence Labeling: Deterministic Regex assigned 0.95; contextual NER assigned 0.70 confidence.

Redaction Engine & The Privacy Safety Gate
Offset Invariance and Automated Governance Safeguards

[SLIDE_SHAPE sh6]
•   Right-to-Left Sorting Rule: Slices text from highest start offset to lowest — eliminates character shifting.
•   Explicit Masking Tags: [REDACTED: EMAIL], [REDACTED: PERSON_NAME], [REDACTED: EMPLOYEE_ID], etc.
•   The Privacy Safety Gate: If OCR degrades or a page fails extraction, status is flagged 'REVIEW REQUIRED'.
•   No Silent Passages: Zero PII detected on an unreadable document NEVER passes as clean.
•   Audit Deliverables: findings.csv, findings.json, and sanitized plain text for every document.
